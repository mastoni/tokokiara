# TokoKiara App Flow Document

## Onboarding and Sign-In/Sign-Up
When a new user visits TokoKiara, they land on a clean and welcoming sign-in page. From here they can choose to create an account by entering their email address, choosing a password, and confirming the password. After submitting the form, they receive an email with a verification link. Clicking that link brings them back to the app and confirms their address before they are allowed to sign in. If they prefer, they may also sign up using a social login method, where they click on a Google or Facebook button, authorize TokoKiara, and allow basic profile information to be shared. Once signed up, the user is automatically taken to the main dashboard. Returning users see a sign-in form where they enter their email and password and click “Sign In.” If they forget their password, they click “Forgot Password,” enter their email, and receive a reset link. Following that link brings them to a page where they choose a new password. Upon successful submission, they return to the sign-in page to continue.

## Main Dashboard or Home Page
After signing in, the user arrives at the main dashboard. The layout presents a vertical sidebar on the left with labels for Sales or POS, Inventory, Contacts, Reports, Financial Operations, and Settings. Along the top is a header bar showing the store name, a search box, and the user’s profile menu with “Sign Out.” In the center of the screen is a welcome panel that highlights today’s sales summary, low-stock alerts, and quick links to key actions. From this page, users click on any sidebar label to move into that feature. A breadcrumb trail under the header updates to show their current location so they always know which module they are in.

## Detailed Feature Flows and Page Transitions
When the user clicks on Sales or POS, the screen transitions with a smooth animation. The POS interface shows a product search field and an empty cart panel. The user types or scans a barcode into the search field, and matching products appear in a list. Clicking on an item adds it to the cart, where they can adjust quantity or remove items. Once the cart is ready, they click “Checkout,” choose a payment method, enter payment details, and click “Complete Sale.” A receipt is generated and displayed with an option to print or email it. Finishing returns them to an empty POS screen for the next transaction. 

Choosing Inventory opens a product listing page with filters for category, stock level, and search text. Clicking “Add Product” brings up a form where the user enters name, SKU, price, tax rate, and uploads an image. The image is compressed in the browser before upload and the description field uses a rich text editor with TinyMCE for formatting, media, and emoticons. After saving, the item appears in the list and a barcode is automatically generated. To adjust stock, the user clicks on a product, enters the new quantity or applies batch adjustments, and then saves. Returning from the product detail returns to the inventory list.

In Contacts, the user sees separate tabs for Customers and Vendors. Clicking “New Contact” opens a form to enter name, address, phone, and notes. After saving, the contact list updates. The user can search or filter by name or balance. Clicking on a contact shows their purchase history, outstanding balances, and any communications logged.

The Reports section starts with a list of report types: Sales Summary, Expense Breakdown, Inventory Valuation, and Custom Report. When the user selects a report, a date range picker appears. The user chooses start and end dates, clicks “Generate,” and the screen displays charts and tables. An “Export” button lets them download a CSV or PDF. They then click “Back to Reports” to choose another.

Financial Operations is accessible via the sidebar. Here the user reviews charges and refunds. Clicking on a specific transaction shows its details and audit trail. They can filter by date, type, or status. When closing out the day, they click “End of Day Reconcile,” confirm totals, and the system logs the closing and resets the POS for the next business day.

Administrators have access to an additional “Admin Panel” link under Settings. In this panel they manage user roles, set permissions on which modules each role can access, and view system logs through the Log Viewer. The Log Viewer lists events with filters for date, user, and type, and pagination controls for navigation.

## Settings and Account Management
The Settings section offers a profile page where users update their name, email, and change their password. Below that, notification preferences let them enable or disable email alerts for low stock, daily summaries, or failed transactions. The subscription and billing page shows their current plan, next billing date, and payment method. They can upgrade or downgrade the plan by clicking “Change Plan,” selecting a new tier, and confirming. Billing details update immediately. A global configuration page for administrators allows setting currency formats, tax rates, time zones, SMTP mail settings, and UI theme colors. Saving changes here applies them across the entire application instantly. A “Back to Dashboard” link returns the user to the main view.

## Error States and Alternate Paths
If the user enters invalid credentials at sign-in, an inline error message appears under the form fields indicating “Invalid email or password.” When a required field is left blank in any form, the field is highlighted in red with an explanatory message. If the network connection drops during a request, a banner appears at the top stating “Connection lost. Attempting to reconnect…” and retries automatically. In offline mode, the POS and Inventory modules switch to a local mode using cached data from IndexedDB. Any sales or stock changes made are queued and synchronized once connectivity is restored. If a user tries to access a restricted page without permission, they see a “403 Forbidden” page with a button to return to the dashboard.

## Conclusion and Overall App Journey
Starting from account creation and email verification, the user steps into a central dashboard that guides them into each major area of their business operations. With intuitive flows for sales, inventory updates, contact management, reporting, and financial reconciliation, they can complete end-to-end retail tasks. Administrators configure global settings and manage user roles to ensure security and compliance. Throughout, helpful error messages, offline support, and detailed logs keep operations running smoothly. Day after day, TokoKiara carries the user from sign-in to sale closures, inventory checks, and strategic reports without ever leaving the app environment.
